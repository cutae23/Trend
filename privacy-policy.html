Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 1, 2026

Kimkyutae ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Trended mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Trended.

1. Information We Collect

Trended does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or profile information.

Trended does not collect or use the user's precise or approximate location.

2. Gemini API Key

Trended allows users to enter their own Google Gemini API key to use AI-powered features.

The Gemini API key entered by the user is stored locally on the user's device and is not intentionally transmitted to or stored by the developer's servers.

Users are responsible for managing and protecting their own Gemini API keys and for complying with Google's applicable terms and policies.

3. AI-Powered Features

Trended uses Google Gemini to summarize and analyze trend-related information. Information necessary to perform these AI functions may be sent directly to Google's Gemini API from the App.

Trended does not intentionally send personally identifiable user information to the Gemini API as part of these features.

4. How Information Is Used

  • Provide and operate the App.
  • Summarize and analyze trend-related information using AI.
  • Improve the user experience and functionality of the App.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the App.

5. Advertising

Trended does not currently display advertisements or use advertising networks.

6. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

Trended does not sell users' personal information and does not intentionally share personally identifiable information with third parties.

Trended uses Google Gemini as a third-party AI service. Information necessary to provide AI-powered summarization and analysis may be processed by Google through the Gemini API. Google's handling of data is subject to Google's applicable privacy practices and terms.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed through the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Trended does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
